The Thread

One kid, one game, and where it led.

Baseball players love stadiums. They love history. They love stats. Follow that love far enough and it stops being about the scoreboard — it becomes about the people the game left out of the record. Here's that path, in order.

  1. 01
    The first game

    Falling for the game

    One trip to the ballpark and that was it — hooked. The green of the grass, the noise, the history hanging in the rafters. One game turned into a mission to see as many stadiums as he could.

  2. 02
    Ages 5–18

    Playing the game

    Thirteen years on the field — travel ball and nearly every high school diamond in North Georgia, plus college visits from Wake Forest and Auburn to Louisiana Tech, Sewanee, and Wofford. The game stopped being something he watched and became something he lived.
